Five takes on the email

Same content in all five: the address, the AI paragraph about the month, the button to the rebuilt report, and the manage line. No change list; the details live in the report. What varies is which pieces of the site each one borrows. Pick one, or point at the parts you like across two.

A · CardB · Report pageC · LetterD · NoticeE · Ink
Option A

Card

The site's top bar, then one card: kicker and the rotated tag, the highlighted address, the paragraph, the button with its sub-label, and the manage line under a hairline.

Why pick it. Reads as one object. The same shell already serves the purchase and watch-confirmed emails.

Option B

Report page

The email is the top of the report page: the highlighted address, the borough line, the fact chips, the gist card with its tag holding the paragraph, and the full-width button on an ink rule like the sticky bar.

Why pick it. Most faithful to what they see after clicking, so the handoff feels seamless. The facts are static building info, not a diff.

Option C

Letter

No card at all. Kicker, the big highlighted address, the paragraph at the report's 17px, the button, the manage line. The home page's hero, as a note.

Why pick it. Lightest and most personal; reads like a short letter about the building. Least branded.

Option D

Notice

A compact card whose bottom edge is the button bar, like the sticky CTA on the report page. Kicker and tag up top, a smaller address, the paragraph, then the full-width button.

Why pick it. Smallest footprint and the button is impossible to miss. Feels like a notification more than a report.

Option E

Ink

Option A in the site's dark scheme: near-black page, light-ink borders, coral button, the olive highlight.

Why pick it. What the site looks like at night. Some mail clients recolor dark emails unpredictably, so it needs testing.
